## What to look for in a Hubli beauty academy

1. **Senior trainers.** Minimum 8 years of professional salon experience.
2. **Small batches.** 12 students or fewer per trainer.
3. **Live-client sessions.** Not optional — these are where confidence is built.
4. **Detailed certificate.** Lists each module, not a generic "completion".
5. **Placement network.** Named partner salons across Hubli-Dharwad.
6. **Hygiene & sterilisation training.** A non-negotiable industry standard.
