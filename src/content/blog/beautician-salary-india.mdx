Real, honest salary ranges for beauticians in India in 2025 — across roles, experience and cities.

| Role | Entry | 2–3 yrs | 5+ yrs |

| Trainee / Junior beautician | ₹12,000 – ₹18,000 | ₹18,000 – ₹25,000 | — |
| Salon beautician (mid) | ₹18,000 – ₹25,000 | ₹25,000 – ₹40,000 | ₹35,000 – ₹50,000 |
| Senior beautician / Specialist | — | ₹30,000 – ₹45,000 | ₹40,000 – ₹70,000 |
| Bridal-skin specialist | — | ₹35,000 – ₹60,000 | ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000 |
| Home-service beautician | ₹15,000 – ₹25,000 | ₹25,000 – ₹50,000 | ₹40,000 – ₹80,000 |
| Salon owner (small) | — | ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000 | ₹1,00,000 – ₹3,00,000 |

Add 15–30% to all rows for **commissions, tips and product retail income**.

## What raises a beautician's salary fastest

1. **Specialise** — pick one of facials, threading, or bridal-skin and become the best in your salon
2. **Build a personal client list** — clients who book *you* specifically
3. **Upskill** — body therapies, lash extensions, Rica-wax certification
4. **Move to a premium salon** after 2 years
5. **Open your own parlour** — the biggest salary jump available in this career
